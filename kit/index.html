Household Budget Tracker — Setup Kit

A budget app for your household: a Google Sheet that fills itself from your bank's alert emails, and a phone-friendly web page that shows where the money goes. You do not need to know spreadsheets or code — your AI assistant does the work.

Easiest way to start: tell your AI assistant
Read https://turleytrevon-png.github.io/turley-household-tracker/kit/handover-kit.md and walk me through it.

The pieces

  • handover-kit.md — everything in one file (start here)
  • prompt-1-alerts.md — Prompt 1: switch on bank alerts (5 minutes, days ahead)
  • prompt-2-setup.md — Prompt 2: build the app (one sitting)
  • bank-click-paths.md — click paths for two banks already mapped

What you need

  • Claude, with the Claude in Chrome extension (Prompt 2 checks and helps you add it)
  • Chrome signed in to the Google account that will own your budget
  • Your bank logins — you type them; the assistant never does

At the end of Prompt 2 the app asks you for a one-word household name; after that your phones sign in with that word plus your passphrase. Nothing to send anyone.
